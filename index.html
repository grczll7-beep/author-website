The Book

The Next Chapter Bookstore

Recently widowed bookworm, Maxine Abbot, wants to heal from her grief and reconnect with her distant family, but when her husband dies she discovers a secret he kept the entirety of their marriage: he owns a storefront in their home state of Michigan, and Max has no idea why. When Max moves back to Michigan to solve the mystery her husband left behind she must contend with the landlord of the storefront, Georgie, who is mysterious and intimidating and knows exactly who Maxine is — despite never meeting before.

Warm, funny, and full of heart, The Next Chapter Bookstore is a novel about second acts and the courage it takes to begin.

About the Author

Grace Naumann

Grace Naumann grew up in the libraries and bookstores of small Midwestern towns, the kind with creaky floors and cats asleep in the windows. She has held most of the jobs a person can have before they figure out what they actually want to do, and she considers this excellent research.

The Next Chapter Bookstore is her debut novel. She lives in Michigan with her lovable dog, Hank, cantankerous cat, Merlin, and a husband who is a mix of the two.

She is currently at work on her second novel.
